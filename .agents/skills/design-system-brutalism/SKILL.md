# Apply Brutalism / The Rebel

Read [the Brutalism reference](../../../docs/design/systems/BRUTALISM_DESIGN_SPEC.md)
and [the system/API map](../../../docs/design/DESIGN_SYSTEMS.md). Keep the shared
charter's semantic and conditional-approval boundaries intact.

Use `system: 'brutalism'`; `light` means Manifesto, `dark` means Underground.
Accent indices are 0 Acid Lime, 1 Industrial Orange, 2 Electric Violet. Choose one
signal for the composition and keep it across expressions; never randomize
individual controls or use signal as automatic success/warning/information.

Use hard rules, assertive frames, reversed headers, stamped selections, controlled
editorial asymmetry and condensed display hierarchy. Keep body/data practical,
fields clean and targets stable. Put halftone/collage in editorial/showcase regions,
not forms. Use heavy angular icons and short mechanical cuts, without flashing,
jitter or delayed critical feedback. Reduced motion retains a static equivalent.

Change reusable React data-slot anatomy and shared mappings, not just an outer
preview border. Preserve upstream behavior/provenance, portals, business handlers,
validation, keyboard/focus and AI transport. Keep native adapter contracts separate.

Check both expressions and affected signals, narrow reflow, semantic separation
and real interactions. Current values are exploratory; record actual verification
and synchronize design references rather than asserting final conformance.
